# ShopSphere Synthetic E-commerce Dataset

Synthetic dataset for the AI-Powered Autonomous Data Analyst project.

## Business patterns
- Q3 2025 has a controlled revenue decline, especially in the West region.
- Electronics contribution is reduced in Q3 2025 in the West.
- Customer behavior and engagement data can be joined with transactions.
- Data contains intentional quality issues for profiling and cleaning.

## Data-quality issues
- Missing values
- Duplicates
- Inconsistent capitalization
- Invalid foreign keys
- Invalid dates
- Negative numeric values
- Zero quantities/prices
- Extreme outliers
- Referential integrity problems

## Tables
customers, products, orders, order_items, customer_interactions, marketing_campaigns

This dataset is synthetic and contains no real customer information.
